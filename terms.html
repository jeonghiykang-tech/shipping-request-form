1. The service

The shipping request form, the compliance check and the pricing information are free to use. Creating the final documents for a shipment (commercial invoice, bill of lading and USMCA / CUSMA certification of origin) uses one credit.

  • One credit covers one shipment. A shipment is identified by its invoice number, shipper company and consignee company.
  • Edits and reprints of the same shipment are free for 7 days after the documents are first created.
  • Credits are valid for 12 months from your most recent purchase on the same credit key.
  • Credits are tied to a credit key, not to an account. Keep your key. If you lose it, email me with your Stripe receipt and I will look it up.

2. Prices and payment

Prices are shown in US dollars on the form page. Payment is by credit or debit card through Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by this site. Your card issuer may charge a currency conversion fee if your card is not in US dollars. Stripe emails you a receipt after each payment.

3. Refund policy

  • Unused credits: refundable in full within 30 days of purchase. Email me your credit key and receipt.
  • Used credits: not refundable once the documents for a shipment have been created.
  • Technical problems: if you were charged but did not receive your credits, or a credit was used and the documents did not appear, email me and I will restore the credit or refund it.
  • Refunds go back to the card used for the purchase. Stripe usually takes 5 to 10 business days to return the money.

4. Your responsibility for the documents

The documents are built only from the information you enter. You are responsible for the accuracy of that information, including tariff classification, country of origin, value, export control classification, USMCA / CUSMA origin claims and any licence or permit. The compliance check looks for missing or inconsistent entries. It is not a classification, origin, valuation or licensing determination, and it is not legal advice or a customs broker's service. For a ruling or professional review, contact me or a licensed customs broker.

The service is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, my total liability for any claim related to the service is limited to the amount you paid for the credits involved.

5. Privacy

  • Your shipping data is kept in your own browser. When you create documents, the request is sent over an encrypted connection to the document server, used to build the documents, and not stored.
  • What the server keeps: your credit key, the number of credits, the email address from your Stripe payment, the dates of purchases, and a one-way code for each shipment so edits within 7 days are not charged twice. The shipment code cannot be turned back into your invoice number or company names.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe under Stripe's own privacy policy.
  • Advertising: this page shows Google AdSense ads. Google may use cookies to show and measure ads. You can manage ad personalization in your Google account settings.
  • I do not sell your information. To ask what is held about your key or to have it deleted, email me.
